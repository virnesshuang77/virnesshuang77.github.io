MyAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyAI ("MyAI", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the MyAI Android application.

Important: MyAI is designed primarily as a local AI assistant. AI inference using the installed Gemma model is intended to run directly on the user's Android device rather than being sent to a remote AI inference server.

1. Information We Collect

MyAI does not require you to create an account in order to use the core local AI functionality.

Depending on the version of the App and the features enabled, information may be processed by the App or by third-party services used by the App.

AI conversations and prompts

Questions, prompts, and generated responses used by the local AI functionality are processed on the Android device when the local model is being used.

MyAI does not intentionally send your AI prompts or generated responses to a remote AI inference service for processing.

Device and technical information

The App may process technical information required for normal operation, such as Android version, device characteristics, available memory, application version, network status, and information required to determine whether the device can run the local AI model.

2. Advertising

MyAI may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about advertising interactions, depending on the user's device settings, region, consent choices, and the configuration of the advertising services.

Advertising data is processed by Google and its applicable advertising partners according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

3. AI Model Download

MyAI may require the user to download a large AI model file before local AI functionality can be used.

The model may be hosted by a third-party hosting service. When downloading the model, the user's device connects to the applicable hosting service. That service may process technical information associated with the network request, such as IP address, request information, and other standard server-side technical information.

MyAI does not control the privacy practices of third-party hosting providers. Users should review the applicable third-party privacy policies for additional information.

4. Local AI Processing

MyAI is designed to perform local AI inference on compatible Android devices.

When the local model is used, the model and its inference processing operate on the user's device.

Because local inference is performed on the device, the AI model does not need to send the user's prompt to a cloud AI inference server merely to generate a response.

5. Chat History and Local Storage

MyAI may store application data, conversation history, settings, and other information locally on the user's Android device in order to provide application functionality.

Information stored locally remains on the device unless the user or an operating-system or third-party service action causes it to be removed, transferred, backed up, or otherwise processed.

6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

MyAI does not require an account for its core functionality and does not intentionally collect the following information for its own cloud AI processing:

  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • SMS messages
  • Phone call records
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos or videos for AI processing
  • Microphone recordings for AI processing

This section does not limit information that may be processed by third-party services, such as advertising providers, according to their own services and applicable policies.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed by MyAI or its service providers may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating the MyAI application
  • Running local AI functionality
  • Downloading and updating AI model files
  • Displaying advertisements
  • Improving application stability and functionality
  • Detecting and resolving technical problems
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

8. Third-Party Services

MyAI may use third-party services to provide specific functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services may include:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google Play services for application distribution and installation
  • Third-party hosting services for AI model downloads

Third-party services may change over time as the App is updated.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by MyAI and to limit access to information to what is necessary for providing the App and its services.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

MyAI does not currently require a user account for its core functionality.

Locally stored application data may generally be removed by deleting the application's data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to Android system behavior and any applicable backup mechanisms.

Information processed by third-party services is retained and deleted according to the applicable third-party service's policies.

11. Children's Privacy

MyAI is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account registration system.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, third-party services, or applicable privacy requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
